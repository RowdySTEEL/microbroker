# Changelog

## 0.30.0 (R30)

- Added event-study reports for eligible positive, negative, and neutral company news decisions. Reports include absolute returns, same-window SPY differences, and reasons that an outcome is unavailable.
- Added synthetic event-study verification and demo commands.
- Added a bounded research collection command that includes SPY prices and sends no orders.
- Prepared a standalone portfolio distribution with a reproducible quickstart and CI configuration.

Earlier milestones included historical and streaming ingestion, news revision tracking, local FinBERT analysis, snapshot replay, risk-controlled Alpaca Paper sessions, and portfolio observation. The original delivery archives retain the detailed development history.
